# Install PCRMS Office on iOS/Apple Devices

The PCRMS Office app is available for iPhone and iPad through the Apple App Store. Follow this guide to install the app on your Apple device.

## Requirements

- **Device**: iPhone or iPad
- **Operating System**: iOS 14.0 or later / iPadOS 14.0 or later
- **Storage**: At least 100 MB of free space
- **Connection**: Internet connection for download and initial setup

## Installation Steps

### Method 1: Direct Link

1. **Open the App Store Link**
   - Tap on this link from your iOS device: [PCRMS Office on App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pcrms-office/id6744886296)
   - You'll be redirected to the App Store

2. **Download the App**
   - Tap the **GET** button (or the cloud download icon if you've downloaded it before)
   - If prompted, authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password
   - Wait for the download and installation to complete

3. **Open the App**
   - Tap **OPEN** from the App Store, or
   - Find the PCRMS Office icon on your home screen and tap it

### Method 2: Search in App Store

1. **Open the App Store**
   - Tap the App Store icon on your home screen

2. **Search for the App**
   - Tap the **Search** tab at the bottom
   - Type "PCRMS Office" in the search bar
   - Tap **Search** on the keyboard

3. **Select the App**
   - Look for **PCRMS Office** by Leviathan Systems
   - Tap on the app icon to open its page

4. **Install the App**
   - Tap the **GET** button
   - Authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password if prompted
   - Wait for the installation to complete

5. **Launch the App**
   - Tap **OPEN** or find the app on your home screen

## First Launch

When you open PCRMS Office for the first time:

1. **Grant Permissions** (if requested)
   - The app may request permissions for notifications, camera, or other features
   - Grant permissions as needed for full functionality

2. **Sign In**
   - Enter your Leviathan PCRMS credentials
   - Your username format: `yourname@leviathan.systems`
   - Use the password provided by your administrator

3. **Start Using the App**
   - Once logged in, you'll have access to all your authorized features

## Updates

The App Store automatically updates your apps when new versions are released:

- **Automatic Updates**: Enabled by default in iOS Settings
- **Manual Updates**: Go to App Store → Your Profile → Scroll to find PCRMS Office → Tap **Update**

To check your automatic update settings:
1. Open **Settings** on your device
2. Tap **App Store**
3. Ensure **App Updates** is turned ON under Automatic Downloads

## Troubleshooting

### App Won't Download

- **Check Storage**: Ensure you have enough free space
- **Check Connection**: Verify you're connected to Wi-Fi or cellular data
- **Restart Device**: Try restarting your iPhone/iPad
- **Sign Out and In**: Sign out of App Store and sign back in

### Can't Find the App

- Ensure you're searching in the correct region (US App Store)
- Check that your iOS version meets the minimum requirements
- Try using the direct link instead

### Login Issues

- Verify your credentials with your administrator
- Ensure you're using the full email format: `username@leviathan.systems`
- Check your internet connection
