# Install PCRMS Office on Android Devices

The PCRMS Office app is available for Android devices through direct APK download or F-Droid. Choose the installation method that works best for you.

## Requirements

- **Device**: Android smartphone or tablet
- **Operating System**: Android 7.0 (Nougat) or later
- **Storage**: At least 100 MB of free space
- **Connection**: Internet connection for download and initial setup

## Installation Methods

There are two ways to install PCRMS Office on Android:

1. **Direct APK Download** - Simple one-time installation
2. **F-Droid** - Recommended for automatic updates (see [F-Droid Setup](#fdroid-setup))

---

## Method 1: Direct APK Installation

This method is straightforward but requires manual updates when new versions are released.

### Step 1: Enable Installation from Unknown Sources

Before installing an APK file, you need to allow your device to install apps from sources other than the Google Play Store.

**For Android 8.0 and later:**

1. Open **Settings** on your Android device
2. Navigate to **Security** or **Apps & Notifications**
3. Tap **Install unknown apps** or **Special app access**
4. Select your **browser** (Chrome, Firefox, etc.)
5. Toggle **Allow from this source** to ON

**For Android 7.1 and earlier:**

1. Open **Settings**
2. Go to **Security**
3. Enable **Unknown sources**
4. Tap **OK** to confirm the warning

### Step 2: Download the APK

1. **Download the File**
   - Open your browser on your Android device
   - Navigate to your company's download portal or the link provided by your administrator
   - Download the `PCRMSOffice.apk` file
   - Wait for the download to complete

2. **Locate the Downloaded File**
   - Open your **Downloads** folder or notification
   - You'll see the `PCRMSOffice.apk` file

### Step 3: Install the APK

1. **Tap on the APK File**
   - Tap the downloaded `PCRMSOffice.apk` file
   - You may see a prompt asking if you want to install the app

2. **Confirm Installation**
   - Tap **Install**
   - Wait for the installation to complete
   - You may see warnings about installing from unknown sources - tap **Install anyway**

3. **Open the App**
   - Tap **Open** when installation completes, or
   - Find the PCRMS Office icon in your app drawer

### Step 4: First Launch

1. **Grant Permissions** (if requested)
   - The app may request permissions for storage, notifications, or other features
   - Grant permissions as needed for full functionality

2. **Sign In**
   - Enter your Leviathan PCRMS credentials
   - Username format: `yourname@leviathan.systems`
   - Use the password provided by your administrator

### Updating the App (APK Method)

When a new version is released:

1. Download the new APK file
2. Tap to install (it will update your existing installation)
3. Your data and settings will be preserved

> **Note**: APK installations require manual updates. For automatic updates, use the F-Droid method below.

## Troubleshooting

### Installation Blocked

- **Check Settings**: Ensure "Install unknown apps" is enabled for your browser
- **Security Software**: Some antivirus apps may block APK installations
- **Storage Space**: Verify you have enough free space

### App Won't Install

- **Delete Old Version**: If updating, try uninstalling the old version first (you'll lose local data)
- **Clear Cache**: Clear your browser's download cache
- **Redownload**: Try downloading the APK again

### Login Issues

- Verify your credentials with your administrator
- Ensure you're using the full email format: `username@leviathan.systems`
- Check your internet connection
- Try restarting the app

### "App Not Installed" Error

- The APK file may be corrupted - redownload it
- Check if you have an older version installed and uninstall it first
- Ensure your Android version meets the minimum requirements (Android 7.0+)

## Security Best Practices

1. **Download from Trusted Sources Only**
   - Only download the APK from official Leviathan links
   - Verify the file source with your administrator if unsure

2. **Disable Unknown Sources After Installation**
   - For added security, disable "Install unknown apps" after installing PCRMS Office
   - Re-enable only when needed for updates

3. **Keep the App Updated**
   - Always use the latest version for security patches
   - Consider using F-Droid for automatic updates

4. **Use Strong Passwords**
   - Follow your organization's password policies
   - Never share your credentials
